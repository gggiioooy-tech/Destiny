# Passive awakening and packet counters — 2026-10-24.2

Sources: [awakened Aris skill/effect table](https://www.namu.moe/w/아리스(세븐나이츠%20리버스)/각성), existing local replay packets, and the retained per-hero source snapshot. NamuWiki contributors, CC BY-NC-SA 2.0 KR where applicable. Mechanics below are paraphrased.

Aris's full-growth rage now grants attack and damage increase at six percentage points per stack, capped at thirty. Previously only damage increased, using the unenhanced two-point value. Death removes the accumulated bonuses; native revival starts ten stacks. A lethal hit is not counted again as a new hit in the resurrected life. The passive awakening activates automatically at full gauge without consuming a normal skill turn or reserving an awakening slot. Activation heals allies before cleansing two debuffs. While active, it supplies non-additive recovery/block reduction, a minimum forty-percent defense ignore for her damage, and a separate defense-scaled response after blocking. The response also reduces buff durations. Passive status and gauge are shown distinctly from an ordinary spent awakening in the arena UI.

Evidence is exported as aggregates only in `aris-awakening-evidence-20261024.json`. Across 26 development replay battles, 114 damaging code-seven actions have a median duration of 767 ms. Every observed owner/packet has one such response, including packets with two to four blocked damage rows. There are 29 zero-duration code-seven rows; these are not all silently classified as fresh activations. Gauge packets repeatedly show active basic-turn net changes of minus 25 (119 observations), active incoming recharge, and retained active gauge after revival. The model drains 25 on an existing active personal basic; activation during the current action is not immediately charged a prior turn.

Rage increments were one per incoming action packet in 468 clean, uncapped observations, including 66 packets with more than one other damage action. One eleven-stack outlier remains in the aggregate rather than being hidden; reset/revival interpretation around that packet is not certified. The engine now coalesces rage and its hit gauge across a main attack and its same-packet reactions. Ordinary multi-hit damage still resolves separately; this does not change every hero's hit-count rules by assumption.

Skuld's audit (`skuld-packet-evidence-20261024.json`) covers 15 owners and 76 new-thread rows. No owner/packet adds more than one thread, including the observed packet with two other-side damage actions. Her fate-thread grouping now uses that packet boundary; the earlier model assumption that an assist always adds an additional thread in the same packet is superseded. This evidence is not extended to every other hero counter without checking its own rule.

Native auras during a truly dead owner's absence, exact cross-source stacking, interrupted/queued reactions, all gauge transfers and general same-frame priority remain incompletely certified. Active-passive retention through immediate revival is modeled; a dead owner's active overlay is suspended until revival. Other normal awakenings still use the existing once-per-battle model. Hit-count packet grouping is not a claim that every passive, DOT and nullification uses the same granularity.
