# Compound buffs and Juri lifetimes — 2026-10-24.1

The named-source refresh path previously matched only the source name. A passive granting damage increase and critical damage could therefore update the wrong entry, grant an incorrect amount, and later subtract the wrong stat delta. Refresh now matches source, stat and positive/debuff kind. Three regression cases failed before the repair and pass afterward, including Gwanwoo's periodic rear buffs after their initial application has expired.

Juri's published [skill table and effect definitions](https://www.namu.moe/w/쥬리(세븐나이츠%20리버스)) specify a three-enemy assist and duration extension bounded by each buff's original duration. The previous reaction used only the triggering ally's targets and capped the result at two. It now attacks up to three living opponents in the three-versus-three model. Her resurrection grants the 24-point assist chance increase as a separate three-turn buff, so it has its own expiration and removal rather than being inferred continuously from immortality.

Duration extension now includes timed stat buffs, positive scalar durations, individual status immunities and regeneration. Each tracks its granted duration; expired effects and debuffs are excluded. A four-turn ring shield cannot accidentally be shortened to three by Juri's extension, and a stronger replacement shield resets its duration ceiling to the replacement's own duration. Extension is distinguished from a fresh grant during the acting hero's personal turn: restoring a turn does not suppress that existing turn's ordinary duration consumption. This ordering is an explicit model convention, not a claim of same-frame server priority verification. Refreshing an effect remains a separate operation.

This change does not certify every simultaneous interaction, mixed-source overwrite rule, or target-selection tie. In particular, the three-versus-three assist case does not prove selection order in modes with more than three opponents.

Tanjiro: the [official October 1 patch description](https://forum.netmarble.com/sena_rebirth/view/10/2080) distinguishes ordinary removal of his delayed buff from forced duration expiration. Ordinary removal now cancels it without a reward; natural/forced expiration grants its nullification and fresh two-turn attack increase. His awakening reapplies it only to offensive allies. Reapplication refreshes the delay rather than stacking the attack reward. Buff block and death prevent grants. Active delayed buffs, regeneration and individual status immunities now participate in buff counting; expired entries do not. The panel-stat aura ledger now includes only selected permanent effects, so discarded weaker passives and expired timed passives are not permanently counted. Exact icon grouping of every compound effect remains unverified, and battle-start-stat imports still lack historical numeric timed-buff provenance; no missing numeric deltas are invented from winners.
