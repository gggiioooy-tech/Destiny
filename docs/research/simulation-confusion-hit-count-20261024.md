# Confusion hit-count model — 2026-10-24

Engine 2026-10-24.20 adds Lin's missing confusion ailment and displays its remaining hit count on battle cards. Full-upgrade basic attack applies two counts at 50% listed chance; the second active skill applies three counts at 100% listed chance. Existing effect accuracy/resistance and dedicated status immunity still apply. Confusion does not cancel reserved skills.

The counter is distinct from personal-turn durations: it is consumed once per logical hit across all recipients, never once per damage component. Cleansing and revival clear it, control-only cleansing does not, and turn-based debuff extension cannot add hit counts. Each started attack latches its confusion state through the remaining hits, including after its last count is consumed; a subsequent cast gets a fresh scope. Counter and assist paths share the counter handling. Fixed passive reactions retain their existing non-miss classification and do not consume confusion.

Evidence: the [developer's July 24 note reproduced by Inven](https://m.inven.co.kr/board/sena/6379/1021?category=%EC%86%8C%EC%8B%9D&p=5) explicitly describes the lingering remainder-of-skill behavior and the decision to roll back its fix. The [published Lin descriptions](https://namu.moe/w/린(세븐나이츠%20리버스)) supply current chances and counts. The existing `simulation-lin-confusion-evidence-20261024.md` records one development replay with three-count application and two-count consumption by a two-hit multi-target action.

Important remaining assumptions: this model uses one weak-point opportunity for a confused cast and lets a successful weak-point opportunity bypass the confusion miss. Exact per-cast versus per-hit weak-point ordering is not established by the available replay flags. Existing attack-before-effect ordering is preserved and not certified for confused casts; before-attack buff removal and HP conversion need further evidence. Fixed-passive and mid-cast cleansing interactions are also not exhaustively server-verified. These limits are shown in the simulation model notice. Implementing the hit-count model does not mean Lin or all simultaneous interactions are complete.
